# Generación mediante imagegen integrada

Dos generaciones nuevas sin imágenes de referencia. Los originales se conservan
en `energy-source.png` y `digits-source.png`. El importador deriva los assets
de juego manteniendo transparencia y usando escalado vecino más cercano.

## Orbe

Production game UI asset: one circular cyan magical energy orb, dark fantasy pixel art. Bright sky blue luminous rim, weathered silver ring, dark teal glass center with subtle swirling energy. Center MUST be dark and clear so white numbers can be overlaid legibly. Perfect circle centered, transparent RGBA exterior, generous margin. No numbers, no text, no background, no extra icons. Crisp chunky pixel art readable at 112x112.

## Dígitos

Production game UI numeral sprite atlas, dark fantasy pixel art. EXACTLY ten separate digits in a regular FIVE COLUMN TWO ROW grid. First row exactly 0 1 2 3 4; second row exactly 5 6 7 8 9. Each digit once. Large simple bold readable serif numerals, warm ivory silver faces and dark navy outline, tiny restrained gold bevel. Consistent monospaced scale, same baseline and height, centered in equal cells, generous empty gutters. True transparent RGBA background. No scenery, no labels, no decorative flourishes, no frames. Designed to remain legible at 18 to 40 pixels tall.
